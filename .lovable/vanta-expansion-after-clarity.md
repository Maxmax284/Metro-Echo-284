# Expansion after clarity — recommendations only

Nothing here is built. Order matters; each stage assumes the one before it is stable in real use.

## Near

1. **Cross-device continuity validated in the wild.** Watch real phone→desk hand-offs for a week. Tune the 3-minute and 12-hour edges from behaviour, not taste.
2. **Gated calendar write-back.** Read-only today. Any write is Plan → Approve → Execute inside the platform, with a receipt, and never silent.
3. **Read-only mailbox sense.** Capture-only, same law as Telegram: it feeds the system, it does not control it.

## Mid

4. **Suggested posture.** Vanta may propose a posture with its reason. It never switches one on by itself.
5. **WhatsApp as capture only.** Nikki's primary interface. Intents surface as drafts; nothing executes from chat.
6. **Shared workspace rhythm.** Manager summaries on a schedule, quiet-hours aware, scope-isolated.

## Later

7. **Spoken output**, once the voice is proven in text.
8. **Deliberate camera input** — a held action, never a background sense.
9. **Wearables**, after posture and consent are mature.
10. **Learned attention**, only after enough real history exists to learn from.

## Still refused, by name

No execution from chat. No automatic side effects. No silent approval. No fifth room. No second chat surface. No automatic AI calls. No cross-scope bleed. No silent posture change. No silent calendar writes. No AI acting while you sleep. No gamification, streaks, or generic analytics dashboards.

These are product decisions, not gaps.
